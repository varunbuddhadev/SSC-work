Section 6

DEST — Data Entry Speed Test

Qualifying test in Tier 2 Paper I, Section IV.

Data Entry Speed Test

  • Duration: 15 minutes (20 minutes for scribe-eligible candidates)
  • Passage: approximately 2,000 key depressions
  • Official standard: approximately 8,000 KDPH
  • Equivalent: approximately 27 WPM*
  • Recommended practice target: 30+ WPM
  • Nature: Qualifying

Official qualifying standard (Para 16.2): maximum errors allowed in DEST are UR 20%, OBC/EWS 25%, all other categories 30%. Some posts, such as Tax Assistant (CBIC and CBDT) and UDC/SSA in CBN, have higher DEST standards.

*WPM is only an approximate conversion. SSC specifies DEST in key depressions, not WPM. 30+ WPM is a recommended practice target, NOT an official SSC minimum WPM requirement.
