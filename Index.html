6. DEST — Data Entry Speed Test

⌨️ Qualifying · Tier 2 Paper I, Section IV

  • Duration: 15 minutes (20 minutes for scribe-eligible candidates)
  • Passage: approximately 2,000 key depressions
  • Official standard: approximately 8,000 KDPH
  • Equivalent: approximately 27 WPM*
  • Recommended practice target: 30+ WPM
  • Nature: Qualifying

*WPM is only an approximate conversion. SSC specifies DEST in key depressions, not WPM. 30+ WPM is a recommended practice target, NOT an official SSC minimum WPM requirement.

7. Topic-wise Weightage — Honest Note

SSC publishes marks per section, not per topic. Questions per topic change every shift and every year, so any fixed topic-wise table would be a guess.

  • Use the section numbers above as your official weightage.
  • For topic counts, tally the last 3 to 5 years of your own PYQs and rank topics by frequency.
  • Each section is timed on its own now, so practise with a sectional timer and decide your question order inside the section.
  • Negative marking is heavy in Tier 2 Paper I (−1 against 3 marks), so skip true guesses there.
